The Journey

It started with a simple passion in 2014. From a humble home-based service, providing manicures with nothing but enthusiasm and a small kit, Nails By Little Boo was born.

Through years of relentless learning, attending masterclasses, and refining our craft, we officially opened our doors in 2019.

Today, we are proud to be a team of 10 professional manicurists. We are a collective of artists who believe that a manicure is not just a service—it is a detail of refinement in your daily life.
